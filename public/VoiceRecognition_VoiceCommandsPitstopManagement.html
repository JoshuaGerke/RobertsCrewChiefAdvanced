In rFactor 2 the tyre types are translated from hard/medium/soft etc. to whatever is available for the vehicle.
That requires quite a bit of magic so you may not end up with the tyres you expect. If that happens Pitstop, next tyre compound will select the next type of tyre or if you're adventurous you may try editing <Documents>\CrewChiefV4\rF2\TyreDictionary.json where you'll find each tyre type and a list of words from the names of tyres available in rF2.
